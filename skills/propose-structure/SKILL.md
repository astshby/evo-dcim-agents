name: propose-structure
description: Propose bounded DCIM structural operations under a supplied immutable work contract; never execute candidates.
---

Read current state and applicable evidence. Propose a small, diverse set of
operations concerning partitioning, module organization, or dataflow relationships.
Do not change capacity, precision, interfaces, technology, or evaluator to obtain
better metrics. Each proposal names its target, parameters, preconditions, expected
cost, and verification stage. Distinguish estimates from measurements. Unknown
operators require development and validation, not immediate execution. Return
candidate data only; controller selection is separate.
